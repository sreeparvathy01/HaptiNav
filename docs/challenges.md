# Challenges Faced During Development

## Ultrasonic Sensor Accuracy
One of the main challenges was obtaining reliable distance measurements from the ultrasonic sensors.
Sensor readings could fluctuate depending on the distance, angle and surface of the obstacle.

## Downward-Facing Sensor
The downward-facing ultrasonic sensor sometimes failed to detect certain surfaces reliably.
Smooth or angled surfaces could reflect sound waves away from the sensor, resulting in inaccurate readings or missed detections.

## Wireless Communication
Transmitting sensor data reliably between the headband ESP32 and the glove ESP32 was an important part of the project.
The data format and communication logic needed to be consistent on both devices.

## Hardware Integration
Integrating the sensors, ESP32 boards, power supply and servo motors into a wearable headband and glove required careful wiring, testing and component placement.

## Lessons Learned
The project helped us understand the practical challenges of sensor integration, wireless communication, embedded programming and hardware debugging.
