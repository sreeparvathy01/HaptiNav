# Future Improvements

## Improved Obstacle Detection
The system could be improved by using more reliable sensors (such as lidar or camera)and
refining the distance-processing algorithms to reduce false detections and missed obstacles.

## Better Downward Obstacle Detection
The downward-facing sensor could be improved or replaced with a more 
suitable sensing method to detect steps, drops and changes in ground level more reliably.

## Improved Haptic Feedback
The glove could use vibration motors or more refined feedback patterns to communicate obstacle direction and distance more clearly.

## Compact and Comfortable Design
The headband and glove could be redesigned to make them lighter, more comfortable and easier to wear for extended periods.

## Battery and Power Management
Power consumption could be optimized to extend operating time and make the wearable system more practical for everyday use.

## Real-World Testing
The prototype should be tested under different lighting, weather and environmental conditions, with appropriate supervision and feedback from potential users. Its reliability and safety should be evaluated before practical use.
