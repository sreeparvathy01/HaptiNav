# Project Origin

## Introduction

HaptiNav is a wearable assistive navigation system designed
to help visually impaired people detect nearby obstacles and
navigate their surroundings using haptic feedback.

## Motivation

People with visual impairments may face difficulties identifying
obstacles while walking, especially obstacles at different heights.
HaptiNav explores how affordable electronic components and wireless 
communication can be used to provide additional environmental awareness.

## Our Idea

We developed a wearable prototype consisting of a headband and a glove.
The headband uses ultrasonic sensors to measure distances to nearby obstacles.
The collected information is transmitted wirelessly to the glove, which provides
feedback through servo motors.

## Project Goal

The goal of HaptiNav is to explore a compact, wearable and affordable assistive
technology solution that can provide directional obstacle information
without relying entirely on audio instructions.

## Development

The project combines embedded systems, ultrasonic sensing, wireless communication
and haptic feedback. During development, we tested the hardware, integrated
the components and worked on improving obstacle detection and directional feedback.
